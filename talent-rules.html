Talent Arena Rules & Guidelines

A shared guide to responsible participation, creative expression, fair competition and recognition within PROOFNEAR Talent Arena.

Launch notice: These are preliminary platform guidelines. Official competition rules, eligibility requirements, deadlines, judging criteria and any prizes will be published separately before each challenge opens. No competition is currently accepting submissions.

01. Respect and Community Conduct

Every participant should help create a safe and respectful creative community.

  • Treat creators, judges and other participants respectfully.
  • Do not harass, threaten, bully or discriminate against others.
  • Do not publish abusive, hateful or deliberately harmful content.
  • Respect different creative styles, backgrounds and opinions.

02. Originality and Ownership

Creators are responsible for the work they submit.

  • Submit work you created or have permission to use.
  • Do not claim another person's work as your own.
  • Credit collaborators and other contributors where appropriate.
  • Do not use copyrighted music, images or videos without the necessary rights or permission.

03. Challenge Registration

Each competition will have its own registration requirements.

  • Read the official challenge announcement before entering.
  • Provide accurate registration information.
  • Follow the announced age, location and category requirements.
  • Submit entries before the stated deadline.

04. Submission Guidelines

Every challenge will specify its accepted submission format.

  • Follow the published file format and submission instructions.
  • Make sure your work is accessible and suitable for review.
  • Do not submit misleading, manipulated or impersonated entries.
  • Do not submit another person's private material without permission.

05. Fair Play and Competition Integrity

Competition results should reflect genuine participation and achievement.

  • Do not manipulate votes, ratings or engagement.
  • Do not use fake accounts to influence competition results.
  • Do not bribe, threaten or improperly influence judges.
  • Follow the specific judging and scoring rules announced for each challenge.

06. Judging and Results

Judging arrangements will be announced before each competition.

  • Each challenge will publish its judging criteria.
  • Results will follow the announced evaluation process.
  • Any review or appeal process will be explained in the challenge rules.
  • PROOFNEAR will communicate official results through its designated channels.

07. Safety and Privacy

Participants should protect themselves and respect other people's privacy.

  • Do not share passwords or private account information.
  • Obtain consent before sharing identifiable content involving other people.
  • Do not publish personal contact details without permission.
  • Report suspicious activity or content that creates safety concerns.

08. Violations and Enforcement

Violations may be reviewed in accordance with applicable platform policies and the relevant challenge rules.

  • Entries may be reviewed if they appear to violate competition rules.
  • Fraudulent or abusive participation may result in disqualification.
  • Serious or repeated violations may lead to account restrictions.
  • Participants will be informed of applicable decisions through appropriate channels.

09. Recognition and Prizes

Recognition and any prizes depend on the official announcement for each challenge.

  • Not every challenge will necessarily offer cash or physical prizes.
  • Any prizes, eligibility conditions and delivery arrangements will be published in advance.
  • Do not assume that participation guarantees a prize or ranking.

10. Updates to These Guidelines

PROOFNEAR may update these preliminary guidelines as the Talent Arena develops. Participants should read the official rules attached to each challenge before submitting work.
